Hi, I'm walking you through Agora Forge, our submission for the Agora Agents Hackathon, built with Circle, Arc, and Canteen.

Agora Forge is an adaptive portfolio command desk for AI agents and humans. It reads multichain balances from Zerion and GoldRush, shows net worth and per-chain holdings like DeBank, and suggests rebalancing when market regime shifts.

Here's the live app at circle dash arc dash net dot vercel dot app.

On the overview, you see live market tickers and a cross-chain command center. Switch Testnet for Arc demo settlement, or Mainnet for real wallet data.

Open Portfolio. Connect your wallet to load net worth, allocation by chain, and a portfolio P and L chart powered by Zerion. Click any chain to drill into every token on that network.

The Adaptive tab detects risk-on, neutral, or risk-off regime and queues rebalance actions, aligned with Request for Builders zero four, Adaptive Portfolio Manager.

On Execute, Circle App Kit handles bridge, swap, and send. On Arc Testnet, fees settle in USDC for about one cent, with sub-second finality, the economic OS the hackathon describes.

Our agent console ties the loop together: read portfolio, reason about allocation, and execute on Arc through Circle wallets and CCTP.

Stack: Circle App Kit, Wallets, CCTP, Arc Testnet USDC, Zerion, and GoldRush. GitHub repo Circle Arc Net. Thank you for watching.
